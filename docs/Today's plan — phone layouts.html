Today’s plan on a phone — layout directions

What makes it messy today: every segment stacks five things — label, title, three separate controls (redo / Use saved / Adjust), a full-size collage with expand icons on every piece, a five-line reasoning paragraph, the change note, and a Confirm button — so one segment is about 600px and a three-segment day is roughly 1,800px of scrolling with the controls scattered between the content. These options change how much is shown at once and where the controls live. The pieces are stand-ins for the real collage.

A · Compact rowsEvery segment becomes a short row: a small collage on the left, title and a two-line reason on the right, Confirm and a “···” menu underneath. The whole day fits on one screen; tap a row for the full-size collage and the editing tools (redo / Use saved / Adjust all live in the “···” menu).
