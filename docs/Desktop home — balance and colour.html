Desktop home — balance and colour

Balance today: the content block (1152px max) is centred in the area to the right of the sidebar, so it sits about 110px right of the true centre of the screen, with an 88px gutter after the sidebar and 88px before the right edge. Because the sidebar already weighs the left side, the page reads slightly right-heavy. The options below move the block left and change how the plan/swipe columns divide the width, alongside different sidebar and card colours. Every frame is the same 1552px screen at half size; the black tab on the right edge is the Mailbox.
